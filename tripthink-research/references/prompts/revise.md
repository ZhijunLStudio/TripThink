# Phase 4: Hypothesis Revision Prompt

## Instructions (prepended with all evidence + the model's original hypothesis)

You proposed a hypothesis. Evidence has now been gathered. Your task: REVISE or RETIRE.

CRITICAL RULES:
1. **Let evidence drive.** If the evidence contradicts your hypothesis, reduce confidence or retire it. Don't rationalize.
2. **Retirement is success.** Abandoning a falsified hypothesis is a GOOD outcome. It means the process worked.
3. **Log your deltas.** Every change must be traceable to specific evidence.
