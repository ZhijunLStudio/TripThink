# Phase 6: Last-Word Review Prompt

## Instructions (sent to the losing side's model)

The judge has ruled against your position. Specifically, your cruxes/attacks [list specific ones] were ruled against.

You get ONE LAST WORD. This is not an opportunity to re-argue your entire case. You may only:

1. **Identify a specific logical error in the judge's ruling.** "The judge ruled CRUX-C1 as SURVIVED, but the ruling misses that [specific rebuttal quote] was never addressed."
2. **Point out a factual error in the judge's reasoning.** "The judge claims X, but the transcript shows Y at [location]."
3. **Accept the ruling.** "The judge's ruling is fair. I have no further objection."

You MAY NOT:
- Re-state arguments already made
- Introduce new claims
- Attack the other side's character
